FOR SMALL VENDORS

Start Accepting UPI in 4 Steps

Follow these simple steps to start using digital payments at your business.

🏦

1. Have a Bank Account

Use an eligible bank account that supports UPI payments.

📱

2. Choose a UPI App

Use a trusted UPI application such as Google Pay, PhonePe or BHIM.

▦

3. Create Your QR

Generate a QR code connected to your UPI ID.

💰

4. Receive Payments

Customers scan the QR code and complete the payment.

STAY SAFE

Important UPI Safety Tips

Keep these basic rules in mind while accepting digital payments.

🔐

Never Share Your PIN

Your UPI PIN should remain private. Never share it with another person.

✅

Check Payment Status

Confirm that the payment was actually received before providing goods.

📸

Don't Trust Screenshots

Verify the payment in your bank or UPI application.

⚠️

Avoid Unknown Links

Do not open suspicious payment links sent by strangers.
