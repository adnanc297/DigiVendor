ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is DigiVendor?

DigiVendor is a Community Engagement Project developed by TY-IT students to spread awareness about simple and useful digital technologies among small vendors.

🎓

Academic Project

Developed as part of a TY-IT Community Engagement Project with a focus on practical technology awareness.

🏪

For Small Vendors

Designed for local shops, stalls, independent sellers and other small businesses.

💻

Digital First Steps

Explains digital tools in a simple, practical and beginner-friendly way.

THE PROBLEM

Why Do Small Vendors Need Digital Awareness?

Many small businesses still depend heavily on cash transactions and traditional methods of managing customers and business records.

💵

Cash Dependency

Customers increasingly expect convenient digital payment options such as UPI.

📱

Limited Digital Knowledge

Some vendors may not know which digital applications can actually help their business.

📝

Manual Records

Sales and expenses are often tracked manually, making it harder to understand business performance.

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Turning Digital Awareness Into Practical Action

The main objective of DigiVendor is to bridge the digital gap faced by small vendors by introducing simple tools that can be understood and adopted in everyday business activities.

Instead of overwhelming users with complicated technology, the project focuses on practical examples such as UPI payments, QR codes, useful mobile applications and digital records.
