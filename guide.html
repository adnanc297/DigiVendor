STAY SAFE ONLINE

Digital Safety Matters

Digital adoption should always be combined with safe online habits.

🔐

Never Share Your UPI PIN

Your UPI PIN should remain private and should never be shared with another person.

🚨

Verify Payment Requests

Check the payment details carefully before approving a transaction.

📲

Use Trusted Apps

Download applications from trusted sources and keep them updated.

👀

Check Payment Status

Do not rely only on screenshots. Verify that the payment has actually been received.

QUICK CHECKLIST

Digital Do's and Don'ts

✅

Do

Keep your payment applications updated, check transactions and maintain regular business records.

❌

Don't

Never share passwords, OTPs or UPI PINs, and do not approve unknown payment requests.
